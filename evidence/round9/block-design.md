## Mechanism selected

**VERIFIED source observations** in the parent `parse.rs`:

- There is no `d_end_best`; the length search is `d_best_len`, lines 2030–2041.
  It computes and compares `((endpoint_cost + length_cost) << 9) | length` on
  every iteration.
- `d_cand`, lines 2091–2106, rejects a candidate unless `prev < len <= 258`
  and scans lengths `(prev, len]`. The node's loop at 2221–2226 updates `prev`
  after each candidate. Those candidate intervals are disjoint, so a claim of
  repeated full-length scanning at a single node would be incorrect.
- Length costs are constant within each length-code segment: construction at
  1880–1884 uses `lsym[length]` and that symbol's extra bits. The current endpoint
  reduction has no range-minimum cache or length-code-segment minimum structure.
  Such a cache is a separate, more complex design; it is not part of this patch.

The selected change removes packing from the inner scalar reduction. A second
variant uses four independent raw-cost reductions, merges their packed minima,
and scans at most three trailing lengths. Search budgets, candidate records,
cost tables, backward pushes, routing, and final output validation are unchanged.
This targets the backward D planner, rather than repeating R7's forward
`relax-contiguous` experiment.

## Why the result should remain identical

**INFERRED from the guarded Rust arithmetic**:

1. Each endpoint cost is `ring[cell] >> 32`, at most `2^32 - 1`. Each length cost
   is a `u32`, at most `2^32 - 1`. Their sum is at most `2^33 - 2`; its wrapping
   addition and shift by nine do not overflow `u64`.
2. In the fast path, `lo < e <= 259`, so every scanned length is below 512. Thus
   `cost << 9 | length` orders candidates by cost first, then shorter length.
3. The scalar reduction retains the first smallest raw cost while iterating
   lengths in ascending order, then packs once. This retains the original tie.
4. The four-way reduction retains the shortest tie within each lane. Packing
   the lane winners before their final minimum retains the globally shortest
   tie. The four-byte groups plus the scalar tail cover the same interval once.
5. Empty intervals and intervals outside the guard use `d_best_len_old`, an
   exact renamed copy of the parent's original scan. The public `d_best_len`
   signature is unchanged, and its callers remain unchanged.

This algebraic argument and finite arithmetic checks are not substitutes for
fresh Rust extraction or end-to-end token/output differential checks.

## Measurement and stop conditions

Block1 was previously about 23.4% slower than #361 for only 0.02426 pp less
output; the new reduction optimizes only part of that extra work. **UNKNOWN**:
whether this part is large enough to recover meaningful frontier space. The
compiler may already simplify packing, and four-way code can increase code
size or register pressure.

Measure candidates against paired block1 for equivalence and mechanism speed,
and against paired public #361 plus its same-byte shadow for the overall
tradeoff. Separate diagnostic timing for `d_parse`, `d_dp`, `d_tally`/cost-table
