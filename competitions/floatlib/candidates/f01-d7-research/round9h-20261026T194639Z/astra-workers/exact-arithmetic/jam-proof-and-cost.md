Write P=2^17(2^k q+t)+u for k=6 or7,0≤t<2^k,0≤u<2^17. Letδ=[u>0]. Then J=(P>>17) ORδ=2^k q+(t ORδ). The new remainder stays below2^k because its maximum value2^k−1 is already odd. Thus quotient and quotient parity are unchanged.

Put H=2^(k−1), which is even. For t<H, the only dangerous t=H−1 is odd and stays unchanged; every smaller value stays belowH even after OR1. For t>H it stays aboveH. For t=H,δ0 gives exact half andδ1 gives H+1. These match precisely the original residual comparisons between2^17 t+u and2^17 H. Therefore RNE(J/2^k)=RNE(P/2^(k+17)). This does not claim k=1.
