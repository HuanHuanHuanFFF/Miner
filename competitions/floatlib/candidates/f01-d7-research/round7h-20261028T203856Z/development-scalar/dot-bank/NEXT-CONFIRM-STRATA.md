# Additional confirmation strata from the fixed coverage cross-tab

This is a plan addendum only. No seeds/input words are selected or generated.
The frozen NEXT-CONFIRM.md and coverage-summary.json keep their original bytes.

The seen corpus has114 model word64 outputs, but108 are zero and only6 are
nonzero (one fixed alternating-sign row against itself in each bank). This does
not exercise a random nonzero narrow-span workload against the strongest signed
UInt64 single-chain reference. In contrast all260 model word128 outputs are FP
accepted;80/90 replay outputs are accepted, with10 replay outputs falling back.
Counts do not quantify their time contribution.

The next predeclared supported-finite engineering corpus should explicitly
include THREE baseline-domain strata, rather than only increasing old seeds:

- Nonzero narrow-span word64: for each selected inner length keep operand
  nonzero exponent ranges narrow enough that48+separableSpan+ceilLog2(n)≤62.
  Include positive sums and separate signed/cancellation examples. Verify the
  expected tier from the generated bytes, not from a desired distribution label.
- Normal word128: hold the corresponding bound between63 and128, including
  well-conditioned sums and cancellations. This tests the main already-seen
  skip-UInt128 mechanism without leaning on reference big-integer replay.
- Replay: deliberately wide span and boundary cases. Split coincident versus
  opposed operand exponent peaks. The opposed case must retain separable H;
  no free paired maximum or data-dependent pair cache is introduced for FP.

Use predefined quotas and keep every generated case and slow result. Allocation
weights are research design choices to freeze before drawing unseen input words;
they are not target workload weights. The formal target distribution stays
UNKNOWN until the user selects a target and its actual source/workload is read.
Include low-row-reuse1×many/many×1 as well as current reuse families, preserving
whole-call preparation/output/drop boundaries in every case.

No threshold, existing source or official gate changes follow from this note.
The current eager speedup is observed on the current synthetic library; a
stronger future corpus may reduce or reverse it, and that result must be retained.
