# Ordered sparse-B compaction: PARK before implementation

The candidate mechanism would compact zero B entries or zero B2 packets once, certify each A row finite, and visit retained k indices in the original increasing order. It would remove actual zero-product MACs, not reassociate nonzero sums. Correctness still needs the reachable +0 accumulator/signed-zero invariant, exact zero multiplication with finite A, and NaN replay compatibility. No such implementation or proof was created.
