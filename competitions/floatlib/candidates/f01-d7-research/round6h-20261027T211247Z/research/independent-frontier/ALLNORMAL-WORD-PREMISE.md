# Conditional high-coverage continuation: normal inputs of both signs

Decision at source/domain stage: **HOLD for frozen same-sign cost and actual public-log2 codegen**. No second runtime/candidate, new random oracle, full proof, or CI. This is a paper continuation justified by the newly located LLVM22.1.4 floorlog2 recognizer, not adoption of a previous negative candidate.

History first: R19 all-normal GRS7737 had actual diagnostic C/ASM, but its hit computed one UInt32.toFloat32 and then a non-tail toBits call, kept original words across the call for postE fallback, and saved6 registers with2 hot stack writes. See round9h/astra-workers/runtime-representation/grs-codegen-40430974047/REVIEW.md. Its MainFull still selected B2; those particular codegen receipts did not measure a new public ADD. Older R16 one-cast full ADD1.8739 and old6bef same-sign pureword bench1.09609 remain negative evidence. The proposed continuation would remove the numeric cast/native codec from normal hits, require no post-round fallback for normal input semantics, and use public integer log2 only where cancellation needs normalization. Whether its actual caller has a smaller frame or lower cost is UNKNOWN.

Input domain is exactly two normal finite words. Sort full magnitude so the chosen sign is the larger magnitude's sign; preserve original x,y for all domain misses. Same-sign arithmetic is the frozen mechanism conceptually, but no source is changed. Opposite signs have a useful mathematical split, for24-bit a,b and exponent gap d:

- d=0: exact significand difference is an integer, and extended difference is a multiple of8. Equality returns +0 under RNE. Any deep left normalization loses no bits.
- d=1: extended difference `8*a-4*b` is exact and a multiple of4. If it needs left normalization, even one left shift makes it a multiple of8; any deeper cancellation is likewise exact. Cases with no left shift still use ordinary final RNE.
- d>=2: `a-b/2^d > 2^23-2^24/4 = 2^22`; thus the27-bit extended difference exceeds2^25, requiring at most one left normalization. The larger exponent is at least3, so this branch cannot underflow to subnormal. Final RNE therefore consumes2 or3 bits of the pre-normalized difference, both already covered by the accepted R19 jam theorem's k2/3 interface. The even-subtraction jam translation itself remains NOT_RUN.

All true subnormal outputs from normal opposite-sign inputs occur in the d0/1 exact branch. Their integer units are at least minsub, so they need exact field packing, not an approximation that rounds tiny nonzero values to0. Normal subtraction cannot overflow above its larger finite operand. These facts reduce the needed machinery but are paper arguments, not a Model.add proof.

Uniform far absorption must use d>=26 for both signs. **Do not reuse same-sign d>=25 blindly:** `1-(1+2^-23)*2^-25` lies below the lower midpoint next to1.0, and RNE produces raw0x3f7fffff, not0x3f800000. The operands are0x3f800000 and0xb3000001, with exponent gap25. This is a symbolic counterexample from the unequal spacing below a power of two, not a threshold tuned against samples. A unified implementation could retain d25 in the ordinary jam branch; no runtime has been written.
