# PARK standalone exact-close ADD; keep the proved paper domain as a shared-adder component

The field formula is valid with both normal finite inputs, opposite signs, **absolute-raw ordering**, gap <=1, and N<2^24. N=0 returns +0; nonzero output uses E=h+elo-23 and N<<(23-h) when h+elo>=24, otherwise the exact subnormal fraction N<<(elo-1). All nonzero normal outputs actually have E<=253. Full derivation, UInt32 bounds and fixed proof interfaces are in `field-derivation.md`.

**One route decision: PARK.** This is a reusable piece of a normal adder whose caller shares decoded fields/order across branches. The present evidence does not justify a separate wrapper or an independent 10%-whole breakthrough. Do not start a full Kernel or CI for this isolated domain. Reactivate only when a broader normal-adder caller supplies the shared fields/order, or a completed actual cost decomposition establishes sufficient eligible-subset savings after miss overhead. This is a resource decision, not a proof that 10% is impossible.
